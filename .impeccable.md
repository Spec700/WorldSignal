## Design Context

### Users

Priority Signals serves security teams protecting VIPs and other important people from physical and digital threats. In the current open-source, local-first phase, operators run the complete application with Docker Compose and use a desktop browser to retrieve hazard intelligence, inspect source evidence, manage credential exposures, and coordinate response work. The interface must support sustained operational use without assuming enterprise authentication or paid services.

### Brand Personality

Calm, precise, operational. The product should communicate confidence and source honesty without manufacturing urgency, looking decorative, or pretending that manually retrieved information is live.

### Aesthetic Direction

A dense dark operations console with restrained cyan status accents, compact typographic hierarchy, strong alignment, and clearly separated control, evidence, and timeline regions. Preserve the existing industrial command-center character. Whitespace should provide scanning rhythm or hold useful operational state; decoration must not compete with the globe, event stream, or dossier.

### Design Principles

1. Keep every globe signal accessible through an equivalent textual control or event row.
2. Make data provenance, retrieval time, cache state, source failure, and uncertainty explicit.
3. Keep counts, filters, timeline state, globe markers, and stream rows semantically consistent.
4. Prefer compact, stable layouts that remain legible during partial failures and dense 30-day views.
5. Target WCAG 2.2 AA outside the WebGL canvas, including visible focus, non-color status cues, and reduced-motion support.
